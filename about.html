Our Products.
>_ Our Vision.

The Push to Main Development Blog is developed and maintained by PushToMain LLC, a company founded by Greg Hudler during the final months of his degree in Mobile Development.

This blog documents the journey of designing, building, and maintaining modern web and mobile applications developed under PushToMain. From the first concept to a working product, we'll share our progress, challenges, solutions, and lessons learned along the way.

Kin Kitchen

Kin Kitchen is a mobile application being built for communities of home cooks, families, and food enthusiasts. The goal is to make sharing recipes and culinary experiences simple while respecting the dietary needs, preferences, and safety of everyone gathered around the table.

Follow along as we build Kin Kitchen piece by piece and work toward making recipe sharing more convenient for families, friends, groups, and communities.

My Concept Cloud

Already available as a beta web application, My Concept Cloud is a platform designed to help storytellers capture creative ideas, organize concepts, and develop those ideas into larger stories and projects.

As development continues, we'll share weekly progress, new features, technical challenges, and the decisions that shape the platform.

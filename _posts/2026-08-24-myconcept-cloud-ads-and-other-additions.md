## The Beginning of Monetization

Its no surprise that this app was made to make money, IU also know that nobody wants to try out an app they have never heard of by buying it first. ***(I mean I did with other writing software that only covered a fraction of what MyConcept Cloud is)*** but io want everybody to be able to use this for free but i still need to cover back end costs.  

## The new additions

### Expanding the workflow on the Novel Creation view

* Added in Image Covers as title pages.
* Added in the Auto generating Table of Contents.
* Improved UX side bar to allow user to create pre drafted versions of popular pages.

![](/development-blog/assets/uploads/title-page-with-image..jpg)

These little improvements are meant to simplify the process of building your books structure so that those just starting out don't mis a page and those who are seasoned can find it easily.

### iOS Updates

In the world of iOS we just added in the SDK for google adds allowing us to prepare for the free version launch in the app store as soon as next month.

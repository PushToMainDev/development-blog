# Inside MyConceptCloud: Everything the Web App Can Do Today

*Where every idea finds its story.*

MyConceptCloud is the home for every story idea — a place to capture the spark, build the world, structure the plot, and produce the finished deliverable, all on every device you own. This is a tour of what the **web app** does right now, in beta.

The whole product follows one simple arc: **Capture → Organize → Structure → Build your world → Write → Share → Track your momentum.** Here's each stage.

---

## 1. Capture the spark — the Vault

Ideas don't wait for a good moment, so capture is built to be instant. Every idea you save lands in your **Vault**, where it's tagged, sorted, and waiting for you.

Each concept is more than a note:

- **Types** — tag a concept as a **Character, Location, Plot, Setting, Joke,** or **Social** idea, each color-coded so your vault reads at a glance.
- **Moods** — give it a feel: *tense, dark, funny, hopeful, tragic, mysterious, epic,* and more.
- **Tags** — free-form tags to group ideas your own way.
- **Variants** — keep multiple takes on the same idea side by side, with notes, instead of losing the versions you didn't pick.
- **Privacy** — mark any concept private, or make it shareable.

**One vault, every device.** Concepts you capture on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch flow into the same vault the web app shows you — and the app even tracks *where* each idea came from, so you can see whether you're a phone-in-the-moment writer or a sit-down-at-the-desk writer.

---

## 2. Organize into Projects

A **Project** is a single story you're building. When you create one, you pick what you're actually making, and MyConceptCloud sets up the right structure for it.

**Supported project types:**
Film · TV Series · Limited Series · Pilot · Web Series · Video Game · YouTube · Podcast · Novel · Short Story · Stage Play · Advertisement · Other.

Each type comes with the right **hierarchy** built in — a Film thinks in *Acts → Scenes*, a TV Series in *Seasons → Episodes → Scenes*, a Novel in *Acts → Chapters*, a Podcast in *Seasons → Episodes → Segments*, and so on. Every level is **color-coded** and consistent across the whole platform, so "Act" or "Season" always reads the same way, everywhere.

---

## 3. Structure with proven frameworks

The blank page is the enemy. Instead of starting from nothing, drop your concepts onto a **framework** — a story spine that teaches you what belongs where.

- **Beat-by-beat guidance** — a three-act spine, a beat sheet, or your own custom structure. Every act and every beat carries a short teaching note explaining what that moment is *for*.
- **Switch frameworks without losing work** — try a different structure anytime. Your content stays in its act and re-slots into the new beats automatically. **Nothing is ever deleted.**
- **Structure insights** — see the shape of your story at a glance, and where it's thin.

---

## 4. Outline your way — four views, one story

Every project gives you multiple ways to see the same outline, so you can work the way your brain works:

- **Structure** — the framework view: your beats and acts with their teaching notes.
- **Outline (Board)** — a clean board of your acts and scenes, drag-and-drop to arrange.
- **Canvas** — a free-form visual canvas to lay concepts and scenes out spatially and connect them.
- **Document / Text** — your outline as a flowing text document when you'd rather write it out.

Concepts from your vault drop straight onto any of these, becoming the **elements** — characters, settings, beats — that make up each scene.

---

## 5. Build the world once — the Story Bible

Stories that share a universe should share a **World**. A World holds a **Story Bible** that lives across *every* project set in it — build it once, reuse it forever.

Inside a World's bible:

- **Characters** — full character sheets.
- **Factions** — the groups, houses, and organizations that drive conflict.
- **Locations** — the places your stories happen.
- **Lore & history** — the canon that keeps everything consistent.
- **Timeline & Eras** — a chronology of your world, organized into eras and arcs, so a story set 200 years earlier still lines up with everything else.

Your world is never trapped inside a single document — it's a living reference every story can draw on.

---

## 6. Produce the finished deliverable

When the structure is ready, turn it into the real thing. Each project can hold **Products** — the actual deliverables:

- **Manuscript** — write in chapters and add the full range of book pages: **Title Page, About the Author, Preface,** and the rest, from a simple dropdown.
- **Script / Screenplay** — an **industry-standard** script editor with proper screenplay formatting: elements auto-format, scene headings and characters auto-uppercase, and the page paginates like a real script. Choose your working method — **Draft, Revise,** or **Table Read** — and keep a character panel that can scope to the current scene or the whole script.

**Export** your work when it's ready to leave the nest — including **Fountain**, **Markdown**, and plain text.

---

## 7. Share & collaborate

Your work is yours, but you can bring the right people in — **per person, as a viewer or an editor.**

- **Share projects and worlds** individually with specific people.
- **Invite by @username, as a friend, or with an invite link** — whoever opens the link joins with the access you granted.
- **Friend "add-me" links with a QR code** — connect with other writers even if your profile is private.
- **Dashboard invites** — see and accept everything shared with you in one place.

---

## 8. Your profile & momentum

Writing is a practice, and MyConceptCloud makes the practice visible. Your **profile** turns everything you've done into progress you can feel:

- **Stats** — words written, ideas captured, and your writing **streak.**
- **Activity heatmap** — your whole creative year at a glance, with the brightest days glowing gold.
- **Where you capture** — a breakdown of the devices your ideas come from (web, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch).
- **Badges** — earnable achievements shown on your profile, starting with the **🏆 OG Beta Tester** badge for everyone who joins during the beta.
- **Your writer identity** — a bio, the kinds of writer you are, and the genres you work in.

Every writer also gets a **public profile** at `myconcept.cloud/u/your-username` to share who you are and what you're building.
